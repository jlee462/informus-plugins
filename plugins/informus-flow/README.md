# Informus Flow, for Claude

Flow in Claude, on your own Claude plan: what's waiting on you, your priorities and work packages,
capturing thoughts onto the right priority, and building from a package in Claude Code.

It connects to Informus Flow's connector. The first time, Claude opens Flow: sign in, pick the
workspace, **Connect**. You can revoke it any time from **Your passes** in Flow.

## Skills

- `/informus-flow:whats-waiting`: what's waiting on you
- `/informus-flow:capture-a-thought`: your words onto the right priority, after your yes
- `/informus-flow:work-a-package`: open a package and move it forward
- `/informus-flow:build-from-a-package`: build a package in the repository you're in

The plugin carries no keys and no data. It only works for people with access to an Informus Flow
workspace.
