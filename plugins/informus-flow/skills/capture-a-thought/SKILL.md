---
name: capture-a-thought
description: Put something the person says onto the right priority in Informus Flow, in their words. Use when they want to note, capture or add a thought, idea, decision or update to Flow.
when_to_use: "When the person says things like add this to Flow, note that on the …, capture this thought"
---

The person's words go into Flow as they said them. Don't rewrite, summarise or improve them.

1. Find where it belongs: `list_priorities`, and pick the priority it's about. If two could fit, or
   none clearly does, ask which. Never guess silently.
2. Show them the priority and the exact words you'll add, and ask "Add it?".
3. On yes, `add_note` with those words. Say where it went and give the link Flow returns.

One note per thing they said. If they're thinking out loud across several priorities, offer to split
it, and confirm each one.
