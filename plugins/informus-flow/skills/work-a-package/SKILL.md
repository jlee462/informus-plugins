---
name: work-a-package
description: Open an Informus Flow work package and help the person move it forward. Use when they name a priority or package, or ask what's next on one.
when_to_use: "When the person says things like open the … package, where are we on …, what's next on …"
---

1. Find it: from `whats_waiting` or `list_priorities`, then `get_package`.
2. Tell them, in this order and briefly:
   - the ask, in the asker's own words (quote them; don't paraphrase a person's words),
   - what it's waiting on and from whom,
   - Flow's open proposal, if there is one, and its next step,
   - what's still open.
3. Help with the next move they choose. If they want to record something, use `add_note` with their
   words, after they say yes.

Don't decide for them. A proposal is theirs to approve in Flow; say where to do it (the link Flow gives).
